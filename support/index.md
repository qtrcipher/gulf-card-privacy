# Gulf Card Support

[العربية]({{ '/ar/support/' | relative_url }})

Need help with Gulf Card? Email
[hamam@gccapp.com](mailto:hamam@gccapp.com) and include a short description
of the issue, your device model, and your iOS version. Do not send passwords,
payment-card details, or verification codes.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all payments. If a completed purchase is not visible, open
**Wallet → Restore previous purchases**. For refunds or billing questions, use
[Apple's purchase support](https://support.apple.com/billing).

## Notifications

You can change individual notification preferences in **Profile**, or disable
all Gulf Card notifications in **iOS Settings → Notifications → Gulf Card**.

## Account and data

To delete your account, open **Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account** and
confirm. For privacy or data requests, email the address above. See the
[Privacy Policy]({{ '/' | relative_url }}) for details.

## Report a problem

When reporting a crash, failed match, or purchase issue, include the approximate
time it happened and the screen you were using. Screenshots are helpful, but
please remove any personal information first.

We aim to respond to support requests within five business days.
